Research: how tipping companies and race models are built
  • Betfair Data Scientists: publish rated prices per runner; "value %" is the gap between the model's win probability and the exchange price. Their Top 5 model is stacked machine learning that scores horse, jockey and trainer ability, 600m sectionals (Punting Form data) and historical BSP, comparing each feature against the next-best runner in the race.
  • Benter / syndicates: multinomial logit over 100+ factors (form, speed, jockey/trainer, draw, going), calibrated probabilities, blended with the public odds, staked with Kelly. Overlay = model probability above market probability.
  • Timeform / Beyer: performance vs master ratings, adjusted for pace and race context; speed figures against a par.
  • Speed maps: built from sectionals, jockey, track and barrier data, separating run style from raw early speed; Racing Life cites roughly 60% of races won by horses settling in the first four (indicative only).
  • Tipster consensus: Bolton & Benter found a nine-factor fundamental model performed almost identically to the consensus of 48 newspaper tipsters, so consensus is a real signal but kept at 15%.
  • Open-source: price-blind CatBoost/LightGBM ranking models with softmax win probabilities, compared with Betfair SP to find value.
Applied here: 5-source blend, pace-shape adjustment, 4-of-5 agreement rule, and ¼-Kelly staking shown on every alert.
Free data sources & all Australian bookmakers
  • Bookmakers tracked (14): Sportsbet, TAB, Ladbrokes, Neds, bet365, PointsBet, Betr (now including BlueBet), Unibet, Palmerbet, Dabble, Picklebet, Boombet, TopSport, BetRight. Roughly 150 operators are licensed in Australia; these are the main racing books. Check each is still trading, and add any other by name in the feed's odds object.
  • Betfair: free historical racing CSVs (daily, win/place, with BSP and winners) for training and back-testing your weights; a free basic historical tier at 1-minute odds; a developer app key for exchange prices (check current terms). Betfair Hub also publishes free rated prices.
  • TAB: its API at api.beta.tab.com.au serves meetings, runners, fixed odds, form and pools; third-party docs say read-only public data uses client credentials. Check Tabcorp's terms before relying on it.
  • PuntersEdge free tier: 1,500 credits a month covering racing prices from 11+ Australian books, plus best-odds and movers. Enough for periodic checks, not constant polling.
  • Free ratings and maps: Punting Form (free speed maps and Saturday metro ratings), Racing Life speed maps, Betfair Hub ratings. Good for cross-checking by eye; automated scraping of these or of bookmaker sites normally breaches their terms.
  • Reality check: no free source gives live odds for all 14 books plus ratings. The realistic free stack is TAB API + PuntersEdge free tier + Betfair exchange and Hub ratings + your own model trained on free BSP data.
